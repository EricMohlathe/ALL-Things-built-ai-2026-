The honest frame

  • Sustained 80–100% win rates aren't real at retail volume (Medallion ≈71%). Small accounts grow on asymmetric R:R + survival + uncorrelated edges compounding.
  • Rank by profit factor / expectancy, not win rate. A 60% / +1.5R system beats an 80% / +0.6R one.
  • Require ≥200 trades before trusting any win-rate number.
  • The legit higher band (≈75–85%) comes from confluence stacking (GM26, ≥4 layers), not single setups.

Backtest protocol

  • One EA, one symbol, tick data, ≥6 months, Conservative.
  • Record trades, win%, avg R, PF (target ≥1.5), max DD, recovery.
  • Retire anything that can't clear PF 1.3 on your data. GM09 (order flow) needs real tick data.
