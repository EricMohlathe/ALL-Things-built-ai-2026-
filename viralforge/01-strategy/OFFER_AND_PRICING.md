# ViralForge — Offer Ladder & Pricing

## The value ladder
```
  Free  ──►  $19 Starter Kit  ──►  $29/mo Pro  ──►  $79/mo Studio  ──►  Affiliate rev
 (proof)      (tripwire)          (core)            (power/agency)      (margin)
```

## 1. Free — the proof
- All 5 tools, **3 generations/day**, watermark on exports, no Content Vault history.
- Job: let them feel a win in 10 minutes so they trust the method.

## 2. Starter Kit — $19 one-time (tripwire)
- A done-for-you **30-day faceless plan PDF** + 50 hook templates + 10 carousel
  templates + the DM funnel script pack.
- Job: monetize cold traffic immediately and **pay for ads on day one**. Every buyer
  is offered Pro at checkout (order bump → $29/mo, first month $9).

## 3. Pro — $29/mo (core revenue)
- Unlimited generations, full **30-day plan generator**, **Content Vault** (save/
  copy/revisit everything), no watermark, all platforms.
- Job: the weekly tool they live in. This is the number that matters.

## 4. Studio — $79/mo (power users / small agencies)
- Multi-niche workspaces, **brand-voice memory**, bulk export/CSV, priority AI,
  3 seats. Job: serve creators running several accounts + freelancers doing this
  for clients.

## 5. Affiliate layer (margin without churn)
- In-app "Tools I use" recommends complementary products (AI voice/TTS, stock
  footage, schedulers, hosting). Rev-share. Never competes with core value.

## Pricing psychology
- **Anchor** on Studio ($79) so Pro ($29) feels obvious.
- **Annual:** 2 months free (Pro $290/yr, Studio $790/yr) — improves cash + retention.
- **Tripwire → subscription** is the real engine: $19 buyers convert to Pro far
  better than cold free users.
- Outcome framing everywhere: *"One client or one digital-product sale pays for a
  year of Pro."*

## Guarantee
14-day "post 7 times and don't get a single saved/share win → full refund." Reduces
risk, forces activation (they have to actually *use* it), and the activation itself
drives retention.
