- **Yoco** — cards, popular with SA solopreneurs.
- **Ozow** — instant EFT (no card needed; huge for the unbanked-card segment).

- **Stripe/PayPal** — international card + USD payouts to creators.
> Offering EFT (Ozow/PayFast) is critical — many SA buyers don't transact on credit cards.

## Legal & compliance (SA)
- **POPIA (Protection of Personal Information Act):** lawful, minimal, transparent
  data processing; clear privacy policy; opt-in marketing; data-subject request flow;
  appoint an Information Officer. (Reflected in the landing-page footer.)
- **Company:** register a **(Pty) Ltd via CIPC**; open a business bank account.
- **Tax (SARS):** register for income tax / provisional tax; **VAT registration is
  mandatory once turnover exceeds R1,000,000/12 months** (voluntary from R50k) — add
  15% VAT to local sales once registered; keep digital invoices.
- **Consumer Protection Act + earnings claims:** never guarantee income; frame
  "$500/week" as a goal/system with disclaimers (done across all assets).
- **Exchange control:** USD earnings into SA are fine via compliant processors
  (PayPal/Stripe/Payoneer/Wise) — surface this as a *benefit* to users.
