# ViralForge — Go-To-Market Plan

## Strategy: eat our own dog food, in public
We grow ViralForge by **using ViralForge**. We run a faceless ViralForge account on
TikTok, IG Reels, and X that publicly runs the exact system the app teaches. The
content *is* the demo. Every post is proof.

## Channel priority
1. **TikTok** — fastest cold reach for this audience; short demos + "watch me build
   a faceless channel with one app."
2. **Instagram Reels + Carousels** — carousels convert saves → follows → DMs.
3. **X (Twitter)** — money-twitter threads; high-intent, links allowed, great for
   tripwire sales.
4. **YouTube Shorts** — repurpose; compounding search ("youtube automation for
   beginners" is our literal keyword).

## The loop (comments → cash)
```
Hook post  ─►  CTA: comment a keyword (e.g. "FORGE")  ─►  auto/served DM
   ─►  qualify in 2 questions  ─►  free win (mini niche idea)  ─►  link
   ─►  $19 Starter Kit  ─►  order-bump Pro $9 first month  ─►  retain on value
```

## 30-day launch sprint
- **Week 1 — Build in public.** Announce the mission ("$0 → $500/week, faceless,
  using one app"). Post the niche-finding process. Drive emails with a free PDF.
- **Week 2 — Proof + tripwire.** Post results/wins, launch the $19 Starter Kit,
  turn on the DM funnel, start a small TikTok Spark Ads test on the best organic post.
- **Week 3 — Scale what works.** Double down on top 3 hooks, launch Pro, add
  testimonials, begin IG carousel cadence.
- **Week 4 — Systematize.** 1 post/day per platform from the 30-day calendar, weekly
  live "build a channel" session, referral ("invite 2, get a month free").

## Paid (only after organic signal)
- **TikTok Spark Ads** boosting *proven* organic winners (never cold creative first).
- **Meta** retargeting landing-page + tripwire visitors with the carousel + IG ad.
- Budget rule: only scale a creative once organic CPL < target; tripwire revenue
  funds the ad spend (self-liquidating funnel).

## Metrics that matter
- **Activation:** % of signups who complete 1 full tool within 10 min (target > 60%).
- **Tripwire conversion:** cold visitor → $19 (target > 2%).
- **Free → Pro:** target > 4% in 30 days.
- **North-star (customer):** # of users reporting a real win (saved post / lead / sale).

## Partnerships / distribution
- Micro-creators in "make money online" niche: give Studio free for an honest build-along.
- Affiliate program for the audience itself (they sell ViralForge to their followers).
