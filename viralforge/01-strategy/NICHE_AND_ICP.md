# ViralForge — Niche & Ideal Customer Profile

## The niche (one sentence)
**Beginners who want to make money online with faceless AI short-form content but
are stuck at "I don't know what to post or how to get paid."**

## Primary persona — "The Overwhelmed Starter"
- **Age/where:** 18–34, phone-first. Core paying market US/UK/CA/AU; massive volume
  in India, Pakistan, Nigeria, Philippines, Bangladesh.
- **Situation:** has a job/studies, wants a side income, hears "faceless AI content"
  everywhere, has maybe tried once and quit.
- **Wants:** a clear, doable path to a first **$500/week**; to not be on camera; to
  stop guessing.
- **Fears:** wasting time, looking dumb, "missing the wave," being scammed by a guru.
- **Watering holes:** TikTok/IG Reels/Shorts, r/sidehustle, X money-twitter, YouTube
  "faceless channel" tutorials.
- **Triggers to buy:** sees a relatable creator post a clean win + a tool that
  removes the part they hate (figuring out *what* to post).

## Secondary persona — "The Plateaued Creator"
Already posts but inconsistent and not monetizing. Wants pillars, a repeatable
series engine, and a DM funnel. Converts straight to **Studio**.

## Jobs To Be Done
1. *"Tell me a niche I can actually win and how it makes money."* → Niche Finder
2. *"Give me 30 days of content so I never stare at a blank screen."* → Content OS
3. *"Write me something that actually stops the scroll."* → Viral Script / Carousel
4. *"Turn the people who comment into money."* → DM Money Funnel

## Positioning statement
> For beginners who want to make money with faceless AI content, **ViralForge** is
> the app that runs the entire system for you — niche, plan, scripts, and sales —
> so you can post today and aim for your first $500/week. Unlike courses and
> generic AI chatbots, ViralForge is an **opinionated, end-to-end money engine**,
> not another thing to learn.

## Messaging pillars (use everywhere)
1. **Speed to first post** — "From zero to posting in 10 minutes."
2. **Faceless** — "Never show your face. Never run out of ideas."
3. **It makes money** — "Content is the bait. The DM funnel is the close."
4. **Beginner-proof** — "If you can answer 3 questions, you can use it."

## What we will NOT do
- No "get rich quick" claims. Aspirational outcome ($500/week) framed as a goal/system,
  always with effort + consistency, never guaranteed income.
- No targeting minors. No deceptive earnings testimonials.
