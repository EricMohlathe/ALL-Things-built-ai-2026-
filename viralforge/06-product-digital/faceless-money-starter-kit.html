Step 5The DM money funnel (copy-paste)

Trigger keyword: “MONEY”. Lead with value, qualify, then offer.

DM 1 — deliver value: “Hey! Here's the free thing I promised 👇 [link]. Quick q so I point you right — what's your #1 goal: (a) stop the leaks, (b) save a cushion, or (c) make extra income?”
DM 2 — qualify: “Love that. Most people stuck on ‘[their answer]' have money scattered across 6 apps. Sound like you, or are you already tracking it?”
DM 3 — bridge: “That's exactly why I built [product] — your whole picture on one screen so you actually stick with it.”
DM 4 — soft offer: “If you want it, it's a once-off [price] (less than the subs we just cancelled 😅). Want the link?”
DM 5 — close + de-risk: “Here you go 🙌 [checkout]. Yours forever, and if it doesn't help in 14 days I'll refund you. Want me to walk you through setup?”

Follow-ups: +24h “Did the free thing turn up anything? 👀” · +3d “Want one quick free win instead?” · +7d “Closing the loop — price-lock ends Friday, happy to hold your spot.”
