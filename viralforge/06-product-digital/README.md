## What it is
[`faceless-money-starter-kit.html`](faceless-money-starter-kit.html) — a polished,
brand-styled, self-contained product the buyer receives instantly. It contains:
- How-to-use quick start (5 steps)
- 5 plug-and-play faceless niches + the best offer for each
- The 30-day faceless plan (rhythm + pillars)
- **50 swipe-stopping hooks** (fill-in-the-bracket swipe file)

- The copy-paste **DM money funnel** + follow-ups

> Open it in any browser. It's print-/PDF-ready (looks clean on white when printed),
> so it can be delivered as an interactive page **or** exported to PDF for delivery.

## Pricing & positioning
- **$19 / R349 once-off** (PPP-localized elsewhere). It's the tripwire that converts
  cold social traffic and **pays for ads on day one**.
- Order-bump at checkout: **Pro at $9/R149 first month**, then standard.
- Why a tripwire: $19 buyers convert to Pro dramatically better than cold free users.

## How it's provisioned / delivered (go-live checklist)
1. Export the HTML to **PDF** (or host the HTML on a gated URL).
2. Sell via **Gumroad / Payhip / Lemon Squeezy** (global) or **PayFast/Paystack**
   checkout (Africa) — instant digital delivery, handles VAT/tax where needed.
3. Deliver the file link in **DM 5** of the funnel ([`../04-content/DM_FUNNEL.md`](../04-content/DM_FUNNEL.md)).
4. 14-day refund honored (low-risk, boosts conversion).
