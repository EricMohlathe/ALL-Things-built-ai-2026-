# DM Money Funnel — Example Output
**Offer:** $27 "Money Starter" Notion template · **Keyword:** "MONEY" · **Platform:** IG/TikTok
**Principle:** helpful first, qualify, then offer. Lead with value, never a pitch.

## CTA line (use on every post)
> Comment **"MONEY"** and I'll send you the free 1-screen money audit. 💸

## 5 DM messages (first reply → close)

**DM 1 — deliver value instantly (their comment triggers this)**
> Hey! Here's the free 1-screen money audit I promised 👇
> [link to the free audit]
> Quick q so I point you the right way — what's your #1 money goal right now:
> (a) stop the leaks, (b) save a cushion, or (c) make extra income?

**DM 2 — qualify + empathize (after they answer)**
> Love that. Most people I help with "[their answer]" are stuck because their money
> lives in 6 different apps and nothing talks to each other. Does that sound like you,
> or are you already tracking it somewhere?

**DM 3 — bridge to the solution**
> Gotcha. That's exactly why I built the "Money Starter" — it puts your whole picture
> (spending, savings goal, and a side-income tracker) on **one screen** so you actually
> stick with it. The free audit is the first piece; the template is the full system.

**DM 4 — soft offer + proof**
> If you want it, it's a one-time $27 (less than the subscriptions we just cancelled 😅).
> You get the template, the 30-day plan, and the side-income tracker. Want me to send
> the link?

**DM 5 — close + remove risk**
> Here you go 🙌 [checkout link]
> It's a one-time payment, yours forever, and if it doesn't help in 14 days I'll refund
> you — no awkwardness. Want me to walk you through setup after you grab it?

## 3 follow-ups (spaced, never pushy)
- **+24h:** "No rush at all! Did the free audit turn up any sneaky subscriptions? 👀"
- **+3 days:** "Totally get it if now's not the time. Want me to send one quick win you
  can do this week for free instead?"
- **+7 days:** "Closing the loop 🙂 I'm dropping the price-lock on the Money Starter
  Friday. Happy to hold your spot if you still want it — just say the word."

## Why it works
Value before ask (free audit), a qualifying question that personalizes everything,
a bridge that frames the paid product as the obvious next step, a risk-reversal close,
and follow-ups that keep *giving*. The keyword ("MONEY") makes the whole thing
trigger-able and trackable.
