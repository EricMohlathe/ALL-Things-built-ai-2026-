## 1) TikTok — primary cold-reach engine

### Ad A — "Watch me build a faceless channel with one app" (founder/demo, faceless screen-record)
**Hook (0–3s):** *"Everyone says make money with AI. Nobody shows you how. So I built the app that does."*
**Body (3–30s):** screen-record ViralForge → type interests → 5 niches appear → click → 30-day plan → a viral script → a carousel → the DM funnel. On-screen captions for each step.
**Close (30–35s):** *"Faceless. Beginner-proof. Comment 'FORGE' and I'll send you the free starter kit."*
**On-screen text:** uses the 32s VO ([`assets/hero-ad-voiceover.mp3`](assets/hero-ad-voiceover.mp3)) as the audio bed.
**CTA:** Comment **"FORGE"** → DM funnel.

### Ad B — "$63 hiding in your phone" (value-first, niche demo)
Full script in [`../04-content/VIRAL_SCRIPTS.md`](../04-content/VIRAL_SCRIPTS.md). Soft-sells the method, ends "comment MONEY". Great top-of-funnel; retarget viewers with Ad A.

### TikTok Spark Ads plan
- Post 5–10 organic variants/week (different hooks). **Only boost proven winners.**
- Targeting: 18–34, interests = side hustle / make money online / AI tools.
- Tier-1 geos for conversions; Tier-2 for cheap reach + tripwire.

## Funnel + budget rules
```
Hook content → comment keyword (FORGE / MONEY) → DM funnel → $19/R349 Starter Kit
   → order-bump Pro → retain on app value
```
- **Self-liquidating:** tripwire revenue funds ad spend; scale a creative only once
  organic CPL < target.
- **Track:** activation %, tripwire conversion %, free→Pro %, and customer-win count.
- **Compliance:** every ad carries the earnings disclaimer; no fake screenshots, no
  guaranteed-income claims.
