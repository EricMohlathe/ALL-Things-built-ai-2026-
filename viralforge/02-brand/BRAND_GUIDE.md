## Name
**ViralForge** — you *forge* viral content (and income) from raw ideas. Strong,
ownable, action-oriented, works as a verb ("forge your week").

## Tagline options
- Primary: **"Forge your first $500/week."**
- Secondary: "Faceless content that actually pays."
- Utility: "Niche → posts → sales. One app."

## Brand voice
- **Direct, confident, kind.** We talk to a nervous beginner like a sharp friend who's
  done it. Short sentences. No jargon. No guru hype, no fake screenshots.
- Words we use: *forge, system, post today, faceless, win, momentum, simple.*
- Words we avoid: *guaranteed, get rich, passive income overnight, secret hack.*

## Logo direction
- Wordmark **ViralForge** in a bold geometric sans (e.g. Space Grotesk / Satoshi).
- Mark: a stylized **spark/anvil** or an upward "⚡" forming the dot/accent — the
  spark of a forge. Monochrome white on dark; spark in Forge Orange.

Signature gradient: `linear-gradient(135deg, #FF6A2C, #FF9A3D)`.

## Typography
- **Display/Headings:** Space Grotesk (700/600) — modern, techy, confident.
- **Body/UI:** Inter (400/500/600) — clean and legible on mobile.
- Big, tight headlines. Generous spacing. High contrast.

## Visual style
- Dark, premium, high-contrast. Orange used *sparingly* as energy, not wallpaper.
- Rounded corners (12–20px), soft shadows, subtle glow on primary CTAs.
- Real UI > stock photos. Phone mockups showing the app. Bold numbers ($500, 30 days, 5 tools).

## Do / Don't
- ✅ Show the product. ✅ Show real, modest wins. ✅ Lead with the outcome.
- ❌ No Lamborghinis/cash fans. ❌ No fake guarantees. ❌ No clutter.
