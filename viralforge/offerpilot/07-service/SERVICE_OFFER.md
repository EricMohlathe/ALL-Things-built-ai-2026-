## Why productized (fixed scope) matters
- Predictable delivery time = scalable + high margin.
- AI does 70% (drafting, tailoring, question generation); the human adds judgment,
  positioning and credibility — which is exactly what justifies the price.
- Clear scope kills scope-creep and refund risk.

## Fulfilment workflow
1. Intake form (current CV, target roles, 3 example job posts, wins/metrics).
2. OfferPilot app generates: ATS scan, rewritten bullets, tailored drafts, interview Qs.
3. Human expert reviews, repositions the narrative, finalises.
4. Deliver via a shared doc + a Loom walkthrough; one revision round.
5. Request a consented testimonial on outcome (interview/offer).
