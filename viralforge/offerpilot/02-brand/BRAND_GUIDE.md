# OfferPilot — Brand Guide

> 🎨 **Figma file:** https://www.figma.com/design/K7QPDGTizYX1n3f5k4ODKf — see
> [`FIGMA_DESIGN.md`](FIGMA_DESIGN.md) for the frame spec and status.

## Name
**OfferPilot** — your co-pilot to a job *offer*. Confident, modern, outcome-named
(not "résumé tool"). Works as a system ("let OfferPilot fly your search").

## Tagline options
- Primary: **"Beat the bots. Land the interview."**
- Secondary: "From CV to job offer."
- Emerging-market: "Get hired globally. Paid in dollars."

## Voice
- **Calm, credible, encouraging.** The user is anxious and rejected; we are the steady
  expert who explains the hidden problem and fixes it. Confident, never hypey, never
  guaranteeing jobs.
- Words we use: *ATS, score, interview, offer, tailored, clear, callback, momentum.*
- Words we avoid: *guaranteed job, secret hack, get rich, instantly hired.*

## Logo direction
- Wordmark **OfferPilot** in a bold geometric sans; "Offer" in white, "Pilot" in the
  cyan accent.
- Mark: a **paper-plane / pilot wing** (career take-off) inside a rounded square, blue→
  cyan gradient. The paper plane doubles as a CV in flight.

Signature gradient `linear-gradient(135deg,#3B82F6,#22D3EE)`. Success green is reserved
for the *win* moments (high ATS score, "interview booked").

## Typography
- **Display/Headings:** Space Grotesk (600/700).
- **Body/UI:** Inter (400/500/600).
- Confident, clean, lots of contrast; numbers (your ATS score) shown big and bold.

## Visual style
- Deep-navy, trustworthy, "fintech-meets-career." Blue/cyan for tech, **green for the
  win**, red used only to dramatise the problem (REJECTED).
- Show the product: ATS gauges, score rings, before/after CVs. Real outcomes over stock.

## Do / Don't
- ✅ Show real before/after + the score moving. ✅ Name the ATS enemy. ✅ Lead with the
  interview/offer outcome.
- ❌ No job guarantees. ❌ No fake testimonials. ❌ No clutter — clarity signals competence.
