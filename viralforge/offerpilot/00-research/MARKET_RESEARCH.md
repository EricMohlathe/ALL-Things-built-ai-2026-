## The decision

sellable résumé kit (digital product), and a done-for-you career service.

## Why this wins
1. **Huge, evergreen demand.** "resume" 128K/mo, "cv" 123K/mo, "career advice" 108K/mo —
   and it never goes out of style; there's a new graduating class every year, forever.
2. **Low-competition entry.** "free resume builder" cluster scores 66 at 13–20
   competition — a wide-open beachhead.
3. **Acute, expensive pain.** People apply for dozens of jobs and hear nothing because
   **75% of résumés are filtered out by ATS software before a human reads them.** That's
   a problem people will pay to solve *today*.
4. **Natural product + service ladder.** Free ATS scan → paid kit/app → done-for-you
   service. The service tier (CV + LinkedIn rewrite) commands R1,500–R5,000 / $90–$300.

## The wedge
> **"Your CV isn't bad. It's invisible — a robot rejected it before a human saw it."**
> OfferPilot makes you *machine-readable first, human-impressive second.*

## Monetization read
- **Digital product (tripwire):** the "ATS-Proof Résumé Kit" — templates + power phrases.
- **Subscription (app):** OfferPilot Pro — unlimited scans, rewrites, tailoring, tracker.
- **Service (high-ticket):** "Offer Sprint" — done-for-you CV + LinkedIn + applications.
- **Affiliate:** job boards, LinkedIn Premium, courses.

globally the kicker: an ATS-optimised CV unlocks **remote jobs that pay in US dollars**.
*Create your CV locally, get hired globally.*
