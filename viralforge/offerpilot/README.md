# 🛩️ OfferPilot

**Beat the bots. Land the interview.**
An AI résumé + career platform that gets job seekers *past the ATS* — plus a sellable
**digital product** and a high-ticket **done-for-you service**.

> Your CV isn't bad. It's invisible — a robot rejected it before a human saw it.
> Built in South Africa 🇿🇦, made for the world 🌍. Get hired locally, **paid in USD.**

---

## Why this exists
~**75% of résumés are filtered out by ATS software** before a human ever reads them.
People apply to dozens of jobs and hear nothing. OfferPilot makes you machine-readable
first, human-impressive second — and walks you from CV → interview → offer.

## The three revenue lines (digital product + subscription + service)
1. 🧰 **Digital product** — the **ATS-Proof Résumé Kit** (templates, power phrases, cover
   letters, LinkedIn formulas). [`06-product-digital/`](06-product-digital/)
2. 🖥️ **Subscription app** — **OfferPilot** (ATS scanner, bullet rewriter, job-match
   tailor, cover-letter + interview prep, application tracker). [`03-product/`](03-product/)
3. 🤝 **Service** — the **Offer Sprint**: done-for-you CV + LinkedIn + tailored
   applications + interview coaching. [`07-service/`](07-service/)
