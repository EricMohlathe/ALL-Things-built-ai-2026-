# OfferPilot — Business Plan (Lean)

## 1. Problem
Job seekers apply to dozens of roles and get ghosted. The hidden reason: **Applicant
Tracking Systems auto-reject ~75% of résumés** for formatting and missing keywords
before a human sees them. People don't know this, can't diagnose it, and can't fix it.

## 2. Solution
**OfferPilot** makes a CV machine-readable and human-impressive, then walks the user
from CV → tailored application → interview → offer. Free instant ATS score hooks them;
AI tools fix it; a done-for-you service closes the highest-intent buyers.

## 3. Who it's for
See [`NICHE_AND_ICP.md`](NICHE_AND_ICP.md). Primary: the **Ghosted Applicant** —
graduates and career-switchers, 20–40, applying online and getting silence.

## 4. Product lines
- **Digital product** ($19/R349) — ATS-Proof Résumé Kit (tripwire).
- **Subscription** ($29/R299 mo) — OfferPilot Pro (the app).
- **Service** ($89–$299 / R1,499–R4,999) — Offer Sprint (done-for-you, high margin).
Specs: [`../03-product/PRODUCT_SPEC.md`](../03-product/PRODUCT_SPEC.md),
[`../06-product-digital/`](../06-product-digital/), [`../07-service/SERVICE_OFFER.md`](../07-service/SERVICE_OFFER.md).

## 5. Why three lines (it compounds)
The free ATS scan is the perfect hook (instant, personalised, emotional). The kit funds
ads. The subscription is recurring base revenue. The **service is the profit engine** —
high-ticket, high-margin, and produces the testimonials ("I got the interview") that
sell everything else. Each line feeds the next.

## 6. Unit economics (target)
- Kit $19 (tripwire, ~80% margin) funds CAC.
- Pro ~$29/mo, ~85% margin, target retention 4–6 mo.
- Service $89–$299, ~70% margin after fulfilment (AI-assisted human review).
- Target blended **CAC < $20**, self-liquidated by the kit; service payback immediate.

## 7. Go-to-market
Content-led: short-form "why your CV gets rejected" + ATS demos + before/after wins.
Free ATS scan is the CTA. Full plan: [`GO_TO_MARKET.md`](GO_TO_MARKET.md).

## 8. Moat
1. **ATS-match data flywheel** — learn which fixes actually win interviews, feed it back.
2. **Outcome proof** — "got the interview" stories are unbeatable marketing + retention.
3. **Service → product loop** — service reveals exactly what to automate next.

## 9. Roadmap
- 0–30d: ship app MVP (scanner + rewriter + tailor), kit, landing, launch content.
- 30–90d: billing, cover-letter + interview prep + tracker, launch Offer Sprint service.
- 90–180d: LinkedIn import, recruiter-grade ATS parser, B2B (universities/bootcamps).

## 10. Risks & mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| "Free builders exist" | We sell *getting hired* (ATS + service + outcomes), not a form |
| ATS rules change | Keep a living rules engine; we sell the system + human service |
| Service doesn't scale | AI-assisted fulfilment + productized, fixed-scope packages |
| Seasonality | Global + evergreen (always a new grad class); B2B smooths it |
