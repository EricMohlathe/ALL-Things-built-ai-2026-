# OfferPilot — Niche & Ideal Customer Profile

## Niche (one sentence)
**Job seekers who keep getting auto-rejected/ghosted and don't know it's the ATS.**

## Primary persona — "The Ghosted Applicant"
- **Who:** 20–40, recent graduates and career-switchers. Core paying markets US/CA/AU/UK;
  huge volume in India, Nigeria, Pakistan, Bangladesh, South Africa.
- **Situation:** has applied to 20–100+ jobs online, hears nothing, feels broken.
- **Wants:** an interview. Proof their CV is the problem and a fast fix.
- **Fears:** being "not good enough," wasting more months, being unemployable.
- **Watering holes:** TikTok/IG/YT "career tok," LinkedIn, r/jobs, r/resumes, WhatsApp groups.
- **Trigger to buy:** runs a free ATS scan, sees a shocking low score, gets the fix.

## Secondary persona — "The Switcher / Returner"
Experienced, changing industries or returning to work; will pay for the **Offer Sprint
service** to reposition their story fast.

## B2B (later) — universities, bootcamps, NGOs
Bulk seats to improve graduate placement rates. Smooths seasonality, big contracts.

## Jobs To Be Done
1. *"Tell me why I'm getting rejected."* → ATS Scanner (free hook)
2. *"Fix my CV so a human actually sees it."* → Bullet Rewriter + Job Match
3. *"Get me ready for the interview."* → Interview Prep
4. *"Just do it for me."* → Offer Sprint service

## Positioning statement
> For job seekers who keep getting ghosted, **OfferPilot** is the AI career co-pilot that
> gets you past the ATS and into the interview. Unlike free résumé builders that just
> make a document, OfferPilot diagnoses *why you're invisible* and fixes it — with a
> done-for-you service when you want a human to take over.

## Messaging pillars
1. **The robot rejected you** — name the hidden enemy (ATS).
2. **Instant, personal proof** — your real ATS score in 30 seconds, free.
3. **From CV to offer** — not a document, an outcome.
4. **Get hired globally, paid in USD** — remote-job upside for emerging markets.

## What we won't do
- No fake job guarantees. No invented testimonials. No "we'll get you hired" promises —
  we improve odds and do the work; outcomes depend on the market and the person.
