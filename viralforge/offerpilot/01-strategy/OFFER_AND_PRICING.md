# OfferPilot — Offer Ladder & Pricing

## The value ladder
```
 Free ATS scan ─► Résumé Kit ─► Pro (app) ─► CV+LinkedIn ─► Offer Sprint ─► Affiliate
   (hook)          $19 digital    $29/mo       $89 service    $299 service    (margin)
```

## 0. Free ATS scan — the hook
Paste a CV → instant ATS score /100 + the top 3 problems. Free, no card. The emotional
"oh no" moment that drives every downstream sale.

## 1. ATS-Proof Résumé Kit — $19 / R349 once-off (digital product, tripwire)
ATS-friendly templates + 75 achievement power-phrases + cover-letter pack + LinkedIn
headline/About formulas + the ATS checklist. Funds ads day one; order-bump to Pro.

## 2. OfferPilot Pro — $29 / R299 per month (subscription)
Unlimited ATS scans, AI bullet rewriter, job-match & tailoring, cover-letter generator,
interview prep, and the application tracker. The recurring base.

## 3. CV + LinkedIn Makeover — $89 / R1,499 once-off (service)
Done-for-you: a human-reviewed, ATS-optimised CV rewrite + a LinkedIn profile makeover.
48-hour turnaround. AI-assisted so it's fast and high-margin.

## 4. Offer Sprint — $299 / R4,999 (premium service)
2-week done-for-you: CV + LinkedIn + 10 tailored applications + a mock interview +
weekly check-ins. The flagship outcome product; source of the best testimonials.

## 5. Affiliate layer
LinkedIn Premium, job boards, upskilling courses, Grammarly — recommended in-app.

## Pricing psychology
- Free scan = personalised pain → high downstream conversion.
- Anchor on Offer Sprint ($299) makes Pro ($29) and the Kit ($19) feel trivial.
- Outcome framing: *"One interview that turns into a job pays for this 1,000×."*
- Service guarantee: a free revision round if not satisfied with the draft.
