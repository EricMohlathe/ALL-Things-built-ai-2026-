Founded in South Africa 🇿🇦, built for the world 🌍. Career pain is universal; the ATS is
the same gatekeeper everywhere; and remote work lets emerging-market talent earn in USD.

## The emerging-market wedge: get paid in USD
An ATS-optimised, globally-formatted CV unlocks **remote roles paying in dollars/euros** —
transformative versus local wages in 🇿🇦/🇳🇬/🇮🇳/🇵🇰. OfferPilot positions itself as the
on-ramp to global employment, not just a local CV tool.

## South Africa specifics
- **Pain:** severe youth unemployment — landing *any* interview is high-stakes. A tool
  that demonstrably improves callback odds has urgent, emotional demand.
- **Pricing (ZAR):** Kit R349 · Pro R299/mo · CV+LinkedIn R1,499 · Offer Sprint R4,999.
- **Payments:** PayFast, Yoco, **Ozow** (instant EFT — essential for non-card buyers),
  Paystack/Flutterwave for the rest of Africa, Stripe/PayPal internationally.
- **Compliance:** **POPIA** — CVs contain sensitive personal data; process lawfully,
  store securely, explicit consent, easy deletion, appoint an Information Officer.
- **Company/tax:** (Pty) Ltd via CIPC; SARS income/provisional tax; VAT once turnover
  > R1m/12 months; B-BBEE can help with B2B (universities, government youth programmes).
- **Distribution edge:** partner with SETAs, TVET colleges, and youth-employment NGOs
  (e.g. Harambee-style programmes) for bulk, mission-aligned reach.
