# OfferPilot — Go-To-Market Plan

## Strategy: weaponise the free ATS scan
Every piece of content drives to one irresistible CTA: **"Get your free ATS score."**
It's instant, personalised, and emotional — the perfect top-of-funnel hook.

## Channel priority
1. **TikTok / Reels** — "career tok" is huge. "Why your CV gets auto-rejected" demos.
2. **YouTube (Shorts + long-form)** — our literal keywords ("free resume builder",
   "beat the ATS"); compounds in search forever.
3. **LinkedIn** — native audience of job seekers; before/after CV posts, founder POV.
4. **X + Reddit (r/jobs, r/resumes)** — high-intent, link-friendly, fast feedback.

## The funnel
```
Hook content → "free ATS score" → scan result (low score + top 3 fixes)
   → email + retarget → $19 Résumé Kit → order-bump Pro
   → highest-intent → $89 CV+LinkedIn → $299 Offer Sprint
```

## 30-day launch sprint
- **Week 1:** publish the "ATS truth" content pillar; launch the free scanner; collect emails.
- **Week 2:** launch the Résumé Kit; turn on retargeting; start before/after posts.
- **Week 3:** launch Pro; open 5 founding Offer Sprint slots (limited → urgency + testimonials).
- **Week 4:** systematise 1 post/day/platform; weekly live "CV teardown"; referral loop.

## Paid (after organic signal)
- TikTok Spark Ads boosting proven "your CV is rejected" hooks.
- Meta + LinkedIn retargeting of scan users who didn't buy.
- Google Search ads on "ats resume checker / resume builder" (high commercial intent).

## Partnerships / B2B
- University career centres, coding bootcamps, NGOs (youth employment) — bulk seats +
  co-branded scans. Smooths seasonality, anchors big revenue.
- Career creators/affiliates: rev-share on the kit + service.

## Metrics
- Scan completion rate; scan → email; email → kit (>2%); kit → Pro; Pro retention;
  **service bookings**; and the north-star: **# of users reporting an interview/offer.**

## Compliance
No guaranteed-job claims. Improve odds, do the work, show honest, consented testimonials.
