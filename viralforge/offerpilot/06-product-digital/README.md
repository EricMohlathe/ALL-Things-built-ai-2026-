# Digital Product — The ATS-Proof Résumé Kit

This folder **is** the sellable digital product (the tripwire in the value ladder).

## What it is
[`ats-proof-resume-kit.html`](ats-proof-resume-kit.html) — a polished, brand-styled,
self-contained product the buyer receives instantly. It contains:
- How the ATS rejects you + the 4 reasons you're filtered
- The pre-submit **ATS checklist**
- An **ATS-friendly template** (copy-paste structure)
- **Duties → achievements** before/after rewrites
- **75 achievement power-phrases** (fill-in-the-bracket swipe file)
- **Cover letter + LinkedIn** formulas
- The 60-second pre-submit ritual (routes into the OfferPilot app)

> Opens in any browser; print-/PDF-ready (clean on white). Deliver as an interactive
> page or export to PDF.

## Pricing & positioning

  traffic and funds ads. Order-bump to Pro at checkout.
- Why it works: someone who just saw a bad ATS score is in maximum pain — this is the
  cheapest, fastest relief, and it earns the trust that sells Pro and the service.
