## TikTok / Reels — primary
**Ad A — "A robot rejected you" (value-first).** Hook: *"A robot rejected you before a human ever saw your CV."* Walk the ATS truth + 3 fixes, demo the free scanner, CTA "comment SCAN." Uses [`assets/hero-ad-voiceover.mp3`](assets/hero-ad-voiceover.mp3).
**Ad B — "ATS score reveal" (demo).** Screen-record: paste CV → score jumps from 41 (red) to 92 (green) after fixes. Satisfying before/after. CTA "Scan yours free."
- Spark Ads: boost only proven organic hooks. Target 18–40, interests = job search, career, graduate.

## Instagram — Reels + Carousels
- Reels = repurposed TikToks. Carousel = "Why your CV gets auto-rejected" (see [`../04-content/CONTENT_ENGINE.md`](../04-content/CONTENT_ENGINE.md)).
- Static feed ad: Canva ad above + in-repo [`SVG ad`](assets/instagram-launch-ad.svg).
- Caption CTA: "Comment **SCAN** for your free ATS score."

## X — high-intent threads
Thread: *"You've applied to 50 jobs and heard nothing. It's not you — it's the ATS. Here's how it works and how to beat it (steal this):"* → 1 fix per tweet → final tweet links the free scan. Run as a thread ad to job-seeker audiences.

## YouTube — Shorts + flagship long-form
- Shorts: repurpose TikToks ("free resume builder", "beat the ATS" — our keywords).
- Flagship: title **"Why your CV gets auto-rejected — and how to beat the ATS"**, thumbnail [`assets/youtube-ad-thumbnail.png`](assets/youtube-ad-thumbnail.png). Compounds in search.

## LinkedIn — native job-seeker audience
- Founder POV + before/after CV posts (anonymised). "I scanned 100 CVs — here's the #1 ATS killer." Drives the free scan. Best organic channel for this niche.

## Funnel + budget
```
Hook content → "free ATS score" → low score + top fixes → email/retarget
   → $19 Résumé Kit → order-bump Pro → CV+LinkedIn ($89) → Offer Sprint ($299)
```
- Self-liquidating: kit funds ads. Scale a creative only once organic cost-per-scan < target.
- Google Search ads on "ats resume checker / resume builder" capture bottom-funnel intent.
