## PART 01 — ORDER FLOW FOUNDATIONS

### What Is Order Flow?
Order flow = raw buying/selling pressure data at every price level in real time. Reveals:
- **Who** is buying/selling
- **How much** at each price
- **Which price** — institutions cannot hide from OF analysis

### Key Vocabulary
| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| Bid | Highest price a buyer will pay now |
| Ask | Lowest price a seller will accept now |
| Spread | Gap between bid and ask — transaction cost |
| Market Order | Executes immediately at best available price |
| Limit Order | Resting order waiting at a specific price |
| Aggressor | Side taking liquidity (market orders) |
| Passive | Side providing liquidity (limit orders) |

### Market Microstructure — How Price Moves
```
PRICE   ASK 1.2005 [SELL 500]
        ASK 1.2004 [SELL 300]
        ASK 1.2003 [SELL 450]  ← consumed by large buy
── SPREAD ──
        BID 1.2002 [BUY 400]
        BID 1.2001 [BUY 250]
        BID 1.2000 [BUY 800]  ← WALL

HOW PRICE RISES:
1. Large BUY market order hits
2. Consumes all ASKs at 1.2003
3. Next orders fill at 1.2004
4. Price "wicks" upward
```
Institutions leave traces: volume spikes at key levels, delta imbalances (CVD), absorption patterns.

## PART 02 — VOLUME PROFILE MASTERY

### Core Concepts
- **POC** (Point of Control): Price with highest volume — "fairest price" — magnetic S/R
- **VAH** (Value Area High): Upper boundary of 70% volume zone
- **VAL** (Value Area Low): Lower boundary of 70% volume zone
- **HVN** (High Volume Node): Dense volume = acceptance, consolidation, S/R
- **LVN** (Low Volume Node): Thin volume = fast transit, no resistance, target zones

### Profile Shapes
| Shape | Description | Implication | Model |
|-------|-------------|-------------|-------|
| D-Shape | Symmetric bell curve | Balanced market | Mean Reversion (Model 2) |
| b-Shape | Fat top, thin bottom | Bullish imbalance | Trend continuation (Model 1) |
| P-Shape | Fat bottom, thin top | Bearish imbalance | Trend continuation (Model 1) |
| Elongated | Thin across wide range | Strong imbalance | Strong Model 1 |
| Double Distribution | Two peaks + LVN gap | Range flip/transition | Context-dependent |

### The 5 VP Trading Rules (Fractal Flow)
1. **POC Reversion**: Price deviates from POC → enter mean-reversion to POC target
2. **VAH/VAL Test**: Price tests VAH from above (failed acceptance) → short toward POC/VAL
3. **LVN Acceleration**: Price breaks into LVN → widen stops; fast-move targets ahead
4. **HVN Stall**: Price trends into HVN → reduce position; expect chop/reversal
5. **Poor High/Low**: Naked poor extreme from prior session → set target at that extreme

## PART 04 — SMC & ICT METHODOLOGY

### Core SMC/ICT Concepts
| Concept | Definition |
|---------|-----------|
| **Order Block (OB)** | Last impulse candle before significant BOS. Returns to OB = institutional re-entry |
| **Bullish OB** | Last red candle before bullish BOS → support on return |
| **Bearish OB** | Last green candle before bearish BOS → resistance on return |
| **Breaker Block** | Failed OB that flips to opposite direction — highest-probability POI |
| **FVG (Fair Value Gap)** | Gap between wicks of candle 1 and 3 in 3-candle impulse. Price revisits to fill |
| **PDRA Levels** | Prior Day/Week/Month High, Low, Close, Midpoint — algorithmic reference points |
| **OTE (Optimal Trade Entry)** | 62–79% Fibonacci retracement — institutional re-entry zone |
| **IPDA** | ICT's interbank price delivery algorithm — price programmed to reach PDRA levels |

## PART 06 — WYCKOFF & AMT

### Wyckoff Accumulation — 5 Phases
| Phase | Events | Action |
|-------|--------|--------|
| A | SC (Selling Climax), AR (Automatic Rally), ST (Secondary Test) | Observe only |
| B | Multiple tests of SC/AR range, institutions accumulating | Map the range boundaries |
| C | Spring (false break below SC) | Buy the Spring with confirmation |
| D | LPS (Last Point of Support), SOS (Sign of Strength) | Add on LPS pullbacks |
| E | Markup — sustained uptrend | Hold, trail, exit on distribution |

### Spring Confirmation (Modern OF)
- Footprint: Heavy sell volume absorbed → classic absorption signal
- CVD: Falls during spring but price barely makes new lows → bullish divergence
- Volume: Spring bar BELOW SC volume (less supply = genuine shakeout)
- Entry: Close back above support. SL: 3 ticks below spring low. TP: Top of range

### AMT Core (Auction Market Theory)
- Markets continuously seek price equilibrium through two-way auction
- Balance (D-shape): 70% of time, mean reversion applies
- Imbalance (trending): 30% of time, trend model applies
- Primary insight: trading breakouts inside balance = ~30% win rate; trading reversion = ~65-70%

### CVD (Cumulative Volume Delta) — Rules
```
CVD rising + price rising  = STRONG BULL (confirmed)
CVD falling + price falling = STRONG BEAR (confirmed)
CVD falling + price rising  = BEARISH DIV (fade the rally)
CVD rising + price falling  = BULLISH DIV (fade the drop)
CVD flat + price rising     = WEAK MOVE (no OF support)
```
