## SECTION 2 — CONCEPT DEEP DIVES

### CVD — The Truth Detector
CVD reveals when price is lying. A green candle CAN have negative CVD (sellers dominated inside the candle). A falling price CAN have rising CVD (buyers absorbing selling). CVD cuts through price deception.

**Bullish CVD Divergence**: Price makes Lower Low, CVD makes Higher Low → institutions absorbing sell pressure at lows → long setup

**Bearish CVD Divergence**: Price makes Higher High, CVD makes Lower High → institutions distributing into rally → short setup

```
// CVD RULES (Production Code Logic)
IF price_rising AND CVD_rising  → CONFIRMED BULL — hold/add longs
IF price_rising AND CVD_falling → BEARISH DIVERGENCE — consider short
IF price_falling AND CVD_rising → BULLISH DIVERGENCE — consider long
IF price_falling AND CVD_falling → CONFIRMED BEAR — hold/add shorts

// Reset CVD at: session open | swing high | swing low
// Relative CVD more meaningful than absolute value
```

### Volume Profile — 5 Rules (Fractal Flow)

| 1 POC Reversion | Price deviates from POC | Mean-reversion to POC target | POC migrates >5 bars |
| 2 VAH/VAL Test | Price tests VAH from above | Short toward POC/VAL | Price accepts above VAH 2+ sessions |
| 3 LVN Acceleration | Price breaks into LVN zone | Widen stops; fast-move targets | Price stalls >3 bars in LVN |
| 4 HVN Stall | Price trends into HVN | Reduce position; expect chop | High volume breakout through HVN |
| 5 Poor High/Low | Naked poor extreme from prior session | Target that extreme | Session becomes extremely range-bound |

### Wyckoff Spring — Why It Works
1. Institutions need low prices to finish accumulating
2. Breaking support triggers retail stop-losses (creating sell orders = supply for institutions to buy at lows)
3. Weak hands exit; strong hands absorb at the lowest possible prices
4. After Spring: institutions have full position → push price up with minimal resistance

**Spring Checklist**:
- ✅ Price breaks below known support level
- ✅ Volume BELOW average on the break (no real supply)
- ✅ Candle closes back above support (wick rejection)
- ✅ Volume INCREASES on recovery bar
- ✅ CVD diverges bullishly during the break
- ✅ Footprint shows absorption at spring low
- ✅ SOS follow-through within 3–5 bars

Entry: Close back above support. SL: 3 ticks below spring low. TP: Top of accumulation range.

## SECTION 3 — 12 HIGH-PROBABILITY TRADE SETUPS

### Setup 1 — Liquidity Sweep Reversal ★★★★★ A+
- **Win Rate**: 55–65% | **Avg R:R**: 3–5R | **Best Sessions**: London/NY | **Entry TF**: 5M–1H
1. Identify clear liquidity pool (equal H/L, prior session H/L)
2. Wait for kill zone session
3. Price sweeps liquidity level — wick through but NOT close beyond
4. Watch for immediate rejection — large wick + CVD divergence
5. Wait for LTF (5M/15M) CHOCH or MSS confirming reversal
6. Enter at first FVG or OB formed after MSS
- SL: 3–5 pips beyond sweep wick. TP1: Nearest opposing pool. TP2: HTF target

### Setup 2 — Order Block + CVD Confirmation ★★★★★ A+
- **Win Rate**: 50–60% | **Avg R:R**: 2–4R | **Sessions**: Any | **Entry TF**: 15M–4H
1. Mark all valid OBs on 1H and 4H (last candle before significant BOS)
2. Set alerts at OB levels. Wait for price to return.
3. At OB: check CVD — must show absorption/bullish divergence
4. Check footprint: bid absorption (heavy sell volume absorbed without new low)
5. Enter on first bullish close above OB midpoint with CVD turning up
- SL: Below OB low (small buffer). TP: Next opposing liquidity pool

### Setup 3 — Wyckoff Spring at VP Support ★★★★★ A+
- **Win Rate**: 60–70% | **Avg R:R**: 4–8R | **Context TF**: Daily/4H | **Entry TF**: 1H/4H
- Highest R:R setup in entire curriculum — Wyckoff Spring at HVN/VAL confluence
1. Identify Wyckoff accumulation range on 4H/Daily (Phase A-B confirmed)
2. Map VP: confirm accumulation range at or near HVN from prior periods
3. Wait for Phase C Spring — low-volume break below support, immediate recovery
4. CVD: strong bullish divergence during spring
5. Enter on recovery close back into accumulation range
- SL: Below spring low. TP1: Range midpoint. TP2: Range top. TP3: Prior HVN above range.

### Setup 4 — FVG Fill in Trend Direction ★★★ B
- **Win Rate**: 45–55% | **Avg R:R**: 2–3R | **Sessions**: Any KZ | **Entry TF**: 5M–15M
1. Confirm HTF trend (Daily must be clearly directional)
2. Identify FVG left after strong impulse in trend direction
3. Wait for price to retrace into FVG
4. Enter when price touches FVG with OF confirmation
- SL: Below FVG low (bull) / Above FVG high (bear). TP: Prior swing H/L

### Setup 5 — POC Mean Reversion ★★★ B
- **Win Rate**: 50–60% | **Avg R:R**: 1.5–2.5R | **Sessions**: Any | **Entry TF**: 15M–1H
1. Price deviates significantly from composite/session POC (>1 std dev)
2. No strong trending structure — balanced/ranging mode
3. Enter toward POC when price shows rejection from extreme (LVN hit + CVD reversal)
- SL: Beyond rejection low/high. TP: POC level

### Setup 6 — AMD Opening Range Manipulation ★★★★ A
- **Win Rate**: 55–65% | **Avg R:R**: 3–6R | **Session**: London Open | **Entry TF**: 5M–15M
- Based on Power of Three (AMD) — London fakes direction before NY delivers real move
1. Determine daily bias via HTF structure and PDRA levels night before
2. During London: wait for manipulation move OPPOSITE to daily bias
3. Manipulation sweeps BSL (if bearish bias) or SSL (if bullish bias)
4. After sweep + rejection: enter in direction of daily bias
- SL: Beyond manipulation extreme. TP: Daily bias target (PDH/PDL or HTF level)

### Setup 7 — CVD Divergence Fade ★★★★ A
- **Win Rate**: 50–60% | **Avg R:R**: 2–4R | **Sessions**: London/NY | **Entry TF**: 5M–15M
1. Price at HTF structural level (POI, HVN, VAH/VAL)
2. Strong CVD divergence visible (price making new extreme, CVD not confirming)
3. Wait for footprint absorption at the extreme (wick + high volume + no follow-through)
4. Enter on close that fails to extend beyond prior bar's extreme
- SL: 2–3 ticks beyond the divergence extreme. TP: Next structural level in opposite direction

### Setup 8 — Stacked Imbalance Continuation ★★★★ A
- **Win Rate**: 55–65% | **Avg R:R**: 2–3R | **Sessions**: NY Main | **Entry TF**: 1M–5M
1. Identify 3+ consecutive footprint rows with dominant one-sided delta
2. Mark stacked imbalance zone
3. Wait for price to pull back and test the stacked imbalance zone
4. Enter on absorption at zone (counter-side appears but fails to penetrate zone)
- SL: Beyond stacked imbalance zone. TP: Prior swing or next liquidity pool

### Setup 9 — Institutional Stop Hunt + Reversal ★★★★ A
- **Win Rate**: 55–60% | **Avg R:R**: 3–5R | **Sessions**: London/NY | **Entry TF**: 5M–15M
1. Identify obvious swing high/low that retail has stops concentrated at
2. Wait for price to spike through (stop hunt candle — large wick)
3. Watch for immediate delta flip: footprint shows buyers appearing after the spike
4. CVD makes a bottom/top while price makes the extreme
5. Enter on LTF CHOCH confirming reversal direction
- SL: Beyond stop hunt wick. TP: Full return to prior structure

### Setup 10 — LVN Breakout (Acceleration) ★★★★ A
- **Win Rate**: 55–65% | **Avg R:R**: 2–3R | **Sessions**: NY Main | **Entry TF**: 5M–15M
1. Identify confirmed Market State: Imbalanced (open outside VA, trending CVD)
2. Mark LVN above/below current price as target
3. Wait for price to approach LVN from below/above with strong directional delta
4. Enter on CVD acceleration into LVN (do NOT wait for price to stop at LVN)
- SL: Back inside the HVN below entry. TP: Next HVN or liquidity pool beyond LVN

### Setup 11 — Absorption Reversal at Key Level ★★★★★ A+
- **Win Rate**: 60–70% | **Avg R:R**: 3–6R | **Sessions**: London/NY | **Entry TF**: 5M–15M
1. Price at known VP level (VAL, VAH, Prior POC) or HTF OB
2. Aggressive selling/buying attempts to push through level
3. Footprint shows: large delta against direction but price NOT moving
4. CVD: trending against trade direction but price barely moves (divergence)
5. Wait for aggressor exhaustion (delta volume shrinking each candle)
6. Enter on first candle where price closes BACK in trade direction
- SL: 2–3 ticks below/above the absorption zone low/high. TP: POC or next VP level

### Setup 12 — Multi-Day Context Fade ★★★★ A
- **Win Rate**: 55–65% | **Avg R:R**: 3–5R | **Context TF**: Weekly | **Entry TF**: 4H–1H
1. Prior week closed at extreme (high or low) — identifies weekly imbalance
2. Current week opens with continuation but into thin LVN zone
3. Map composite weekly VP — price near weekly POC from below (resistance) or above (support)
4. Identify clustering of: weekly VAH/VAL + prior session POC + LVN
5. Enter at triple confluence with CVD divergence confirmation
- SL: Tight — beyond weekly LVN. TP1: Weekly POC. TP2: Weekly VAL/VAH (opposite)

## SECTION 4 — RISK MANAGEMENT FRAMEWORKS

### Framework 1 — Universal Position Sizing
```
Risk_Amount = AccountBalance × Risk%
Pip_Value = LotSize × PipSize
Lots = Risk_Amount ÷ (SL_Pips × Pip_Value_per_Lot)

// Example: $10,000 account, 1% risk, 20-pip SL, $10/pip
Risk = 10,000 × 0.01 = $100
Lots = 100 ÷ (20 × 10) = 0.50 lots
```

### Framework 2 — Expectancy Model
```
Expectancy = (WR × AvgWin) − (LR × AvgLoss)
// Where LR = 1 − WR
// Minimum viable: E > 0
// Target: E > 0.5R

// Strong example: 45% WR, 3R avg win, 1R avg loss
E = (0.45 × 3) − (0.55 × 1) = 1.35 − 0.55 = +0.80R ✅
```

## SECTION 5 — SELF-ASSESSMENT (40 QUESTIONS — KEY ANSWERS)

**Q1**: Green candle + declining CVD = ?
**A**: Bearish CVD divergence — sellers absorbing rally. High-probability short at resistance.

**Q2**: Price approaches LVN in bullish trend. Expected behavior?
**A**: Accelerate rapidly through the LVN (no resistance in trend direction). Use as profit zone.

**Q3**: 15M CHOCH against Daily bullish trend — action?
**A**: Check 4H — if still bullish, treat as pullback/liquidity grab opportunity, NOT reversal.

**Q4**: Breaker Block vs standard Order Block?
**A**: Breaker Block = failed OB violated then flipping to opposite direction — highest-probability POI.

**Q5**: Why is the Wyckoff Spring superior for entries?
**A**: Provides lowest risk entry — SL just below spring low (specific level), TP = entire range above. R:R of 4:1 to 8:1.

**Q6**: Bookmap large bubble at ask repeatedly refreshing = ?
**A**: Either Iceberg Order (large hidden institutional buy) OR Spoofing (fake). Context distinguishes.

**Q7**: Price inside prior day Value Area at open = ?
**A**: Balanced day expected. Activate Model 2 (Mean Reversion). Target prior POC.

**Q8**: CVD spikes aggressively down while price barely falls = ?
**A**: Bullish divergence — buyers are absorbing strong institutional selling. Potential long setup.

**Q9**: Stacked imbalances on footprint = ?
**A**: 3+ consecutive rows with dominant one-sided delta = institutional aggression. Trend continuation signal.

**Q10**: Equal Highs forming on a pair = ?
**A**: Concentrated BSL pool above. WILL be swept before major directional move. Mark the level.
