3. Confirming the Spring with Modern OF

Wyckoff Spring + Order Flow Confirmation

Wyckoff didn't have footprint charts or CVD. Modern traders do. Adding OF confirmation to the Spring dramatically increases accuracy:

Footprint at the Spring: Heavy sell volume absorbed → classic absorption signal. Buyers defending the level.
CVD during Spring: CVD falls (sellers active) but price barely new lows → bullish divergence.
Volume: Spring bar volume should be BELOW the SC volume (less supply than the climax). If Spring has more volume than SC → genuine breakdown, not Spring.

Classic Spring checklist: below SC low → close back above → below-average volume → CVD divergence → BUY on close back above
