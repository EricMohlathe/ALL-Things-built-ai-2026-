## ASSIMILATED NOTES

### KB-001 · Creative & channel intelligence, mid-2026 (live-web sweep, 2026-06-11)
**Framework:** Organic-first validation → paid only on proven creative.
- **UGC-style beats polished:** ~4× higher CTR, ~50% lower CPC, ~29% higher conversions (agency-reported — Stackmatix, Influee). Winning TikTok UGC reformats to Meta Reels/Stories.
- **Angles working now:** problem→solution, testimonial/social proof, comparison, first-person story, behind-the-scenes; street-interview format "kept working through 2026" (NewForm).
- **Hook discipline:** pattern-interrupt openers; cut 3+ hooks per video body, run simultaneously 48–72h, judge on thumb-stop + completion (TSL Agency, Creatify).
- **Price band:** TikTok Shop conversion peaks ≈ **$16–32** retail (Darkroom). Hero product should sit in this band.
- **Budget reality for $300–600/mo:** consensus = post organic, scale only the video earning "where do I buy this" comments; then $20–30/day per ad set. Hybrid organic + $10–15/day retargeting claimed 2–3× better ROAS than cold (Teemdrop, TikAdTools). Counterpoint logged: some sources say serious paid testing wants $50/day — which is exactly why organic-first is our path.
- **June 2026 cultural hooks:** FIFA World Cup (started Jun 11) · "Sorry, my hands are full" product-stacking trend (brand-friendly) · **July 4, 2026 = America's 250th anniversary** — record patriotic-merch spending expected; June is the prep window (NewEngen, Ramdam, Printify).
- **Macro:** #TikTokMadeMeBuyIt 30M+ videos; 25–34s = largest TikTok shopper cohort (29%, YouGov). CPCs up ~63% since 2020 → saturated niches are an unwinnable bidding war on a small budget (ScaleOrder/Dropbuild).
**Mistakes to avoid:** posture correctors, galaxy/LED projectors, generic pet toys, watches, supplements, generic gadgets/apparel — all flagged saturated or high-risk by 2026 sources.
**Checklist:** every creative = UGC-style · 3 hook variants · $16–32 hero pricing · organic 1–2 wks before paid · World Cup/America-250 hooks where angle-compatible.

### KB-002 · Unit-economics & saturation intelligence, mid-2026 (live-web sweep, 2026-06-11)
**Framework:** landed cost decides viability, not product cost.
- **The low-ticket AliExpress flip is dead** (2026 sources nearly unanimous): $2–10 gadget flips (sunset lamps, ice rollers, posture correctors, cleaning gel) are saturated. Survivors run branded stores + US/EU warehouses (CJ stocks 400k+ SKUs domestically) or higher tickets.
- **Margin honesty:** "85–95% margin" claims are product-cost-only. After US shipping + payment fees + ads (20–40% of revenue), realistic net = **25–35%**. Most honest dataset found: neck fan $24.85 landed → $39.85 retail = ~38% gross.
- **The battery cluster is the #1 recurring risk:** neck fans, grooming/car vacuums, scalp massagers, zappers, LED masks — lithium shipping restrictions + elevated returns. Prefer no-battery products for store #1.
- **Tariffs/freight now cited as primary margin drivers** for bulky goods (organizers, cooling blankets) — quote landed, always.
- **Compliance hot spots:** posture correctors (documented fake-claim exposés → Meta-ad radioactive) · MagSafe (Apple patent US 10,780,127 + TM takedowns) · baby items (CPSC/food-grade certs most suppliers can't provide) · beauty devices (FDA/medical-claim exposure).
- **CPC context:** dropshipping ad CPCs reported +63% since 2020 → saturated niches = unwinnable bidding war on $300–600/mo.
**Checklist:** live CJ landed quote before any GO · no-battery preference · certs verified for regulated categories · breakeven ROAS computed on landed numbers only.
